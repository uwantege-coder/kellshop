Why we do it this way

Rwandan coffee is mostly grown by families with a few hundred trees. When a roaster buys from them directly, the price they receive can be several times the commodity rate, and it arrives before the next planting season.

We keep our batches small so every roast gets full attention. Each bag is stamped with its roast date and the washing station where the cherries were processed, so you always know what is in your cup.
